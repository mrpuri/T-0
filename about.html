About

Independent advice for money moving on-chain

T+0 advises institutions in the US and the UAE on stablecoins, tokenized financial products and the treasury operations behind them. We're paid by our clients for advice, not by the vendors they choose.

Our view

Cash moves first. Funds follow.

Institutions don't tokenize everything at once. They start by moving cash on-chain with a stablecoin. Then they hold that cash in a tokenized money market fund instead of leaving it idle. Then they put index funds, bonds and structured products on the same rails.

We built the firm around that ladder. We help clients take the first two steps well, then the third. Each step also creates new treasury, accounting and control work, so our third practice stays with clients long after launch.

The name comes from settlement. T+2 and T+1 count the days between a trade and its settlement. On-chain, cash and assets can settle in the same moment, so the gap is zero.

Independence
