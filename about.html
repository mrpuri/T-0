About

We help institutions move into digital assets

T+0 is a US-based consulting firm for banks, asset managers and custodians. We own the client relationship and the outcome, and we deliver through a network of proven engineering partners.

Our view

Settlement is becoming instant. Banks still need to connect to it.

Tokenized funds, treasuries and private credit already settle on-chain in the same moment, around the clock. The infrastructure exists. What most institutions lack is a way to connect it to the core banking, custody, compliance and reporting systems they depend on today.

That connecting work is our business. We map your existing systems to the new market infrastructure, then deliver the product end to end. We start with asset and wealth managers tokenizing funds, where the first wave of adoption is happening now, and expand into private credit, real estate funds and commodities as the market matures.

We are platform-neutral. Our delivery partners bring production engineering and live references, and we remain free to use whichever standard, network or vendor fits each client.
