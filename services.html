Services

Six services, one team from strategy to production

Tokenization strategy

  • Target operating model and partner landscape
  • Board and committee-ready recommendation

Regulatory & compliance design

Map every product to the rules that apply, and build controls into the token itself.

  • KYC, AML and Travel Rule control design
  • Transfer restrictions and allowlists coded into the token
  • Reporting and audit-trail requirements

Chain: identity & permissioning

Architecture & platform selection

Pick the network, standard and vendors that fit your risk appetite.

  • Public, permissioned and hybrid network assessment

  • Reference architecture and security model
ConnectsBank: core banking, custody
Chain: networks, standards, interoperability

Core systems integration

Connect ledgers, payments and reporting to on-chain events.
