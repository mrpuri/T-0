How the money moves

Two lifecycles, and where we work in each

Every stablecoin and tokenized fund follows a path from cash to token and back. The green steps are where institutions most often need an independent adviser.

Stablecoin lifecycle

Dollars in, tokens out, and back again at par.

  3. Holder

    Pays dollars

    A business or person buys stablecoins with cash.

  4. Distributor

    Passes cash to the issuer

    Exchanges, wallets, payment firms and banks.

  5. Issuer

    Mints tokens 1:1

    The same number of tokens as dollars received.

  6. Reserve manager

    Holds the reserves

    Cash at a reserve bank, plus T-bills, repo or a government MMF.

  7. Users

    Pay wallet to wallet

    Payments settle in seconds, around the clock.

  8. Issuer

    Redeems at par

    Tokens are burned and dollars leave the reserves.

Tokenized fund lifecycle

One token per fund share, redeemed at NAV.

  3. Investor

    Subscribes

    With cash or with stablecoins.

  4. Transfer agent

    Records the shares

    A transfer or tokenization agent keeps the register.

  5. Fund sponsor

    Buys the assets

    The fund invests, and a custodian holds the assets.

  6. Agent

    Mints to allowlisted wallets

    Only eligible investors can hold or receive the token.

  7. Markets

    Trades 24/7 or posts as collateral

    For ETFs, someone must design how on-chain price tracks NAV.

  8. Investor

    Redeems at NAV

    Tokens are burned and cash returns.

Why now

The rules are written. Now institutions have to launch.

The US federal stablecoin law takes effect in January 2027, and the UAE central bank has already approved bank-backed dirham stablecoins. Institutions are deciding this year whether to issue, integrate or hold.

$287B

Stablecoins in circulation in November 2026. Two issuers hold about 85% of the market.

$14B

Held in tokenized Treasury and money market funds, up about 60% in a year across more than 120 products.

18 Jan 2027

The GENIUS Act takes effect, setting who may issue a US payment stablecoin and how it must be backed.

Sources: DefiLlama, 12 Nov 2026 · rwa.xyz, 12 Nov 2026 · OCC Bulletin 2026-50 and Pub. L. 119-27. On-chain figures move daily and vary by tracker.
