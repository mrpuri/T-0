Practices

Three practices, one independent firm

We cover the two instruments institutions are putting on-chain first, stablecoins and tokenized funds, and the finance operations both of them create.

01

Stablecoins

For payment firms, banks, would-be issuers and the platforms that serve them.

Decide whether to issue, integrate or simply accept a stablecoin, then get it licensed, backed and distributed.

  • Issue-or-integrate decision and business case
  • Issuer selection and RFP for white-label or partner issuance
  • Licensing path under the US federal regime or UAE rules
  • Reserve composition, custody and redemption design
  • Distribution, listing and acceptance plans
  • On- and off-ramp and payment-rail selection

03

On-chain treasury & finance operations

For CFOs, controllers and fund and bank operations teams holding on-chain cash.

Run on-chain cash and tokenized assets with the same discipline as the rest of the balance sheet, long after launch. The rules are moving: in August 2026 the FASB proposed guidance on when a stablecoin can count as a cash equivalent.

  • Treasury policy: what to hold, where, and which tokenized MMF for idle balances
  • Sub-ledger selection and reconciliation to the general ledger
  • Controls design and audit readiness
  • Accounting treatment and regulatory reporting
  • ISO 20022 mapping and intraday liquidity reporting
  • Reconciliation and incident playbooks for live products
